# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Welcome to OctoAcme Project Management

This directory contains the complete guide to how OctoAcme runs projects. Whether you're starting a new initiative, planning a release, or improving team processes, you'll find the guidance you need here.

## OctoAcme Project Management Overview

OctoAcme uses a structured, customer-focused approach to project management that emphasizes iterative delivery, clear ownership, and data-informed decision-making. Our methodology is built on the following core principles:

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has named Project Managers and Product Leads with defined accountabilities
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback, learning, and continuous improvement

## Project Lifecycle at OctoAcme

Our project management approach follows five key phases:

1. **Initiation** — Validate business need, identify stakeholders, define success metrics, and confirm resource availability
2. **Planning** — Break work into shippable increments, establish acceptance criteria, identify dependencies and risks
3. **Execution** — Build, test, review, and iterate with regular standups, demos, and quality gates
4. **Release** — Deploy to production with proper verification, monitoring, and rollback planning
5. **Retrospective** — Capture learnings, celebrate improvements, and convert insights into continuous process enhancements

## Core Team Roles

OctoAcme projects are staffed with clearly defined roles and responsibilities:

- **Project Manager (PM)**: Coordinates delivery, manages schedules, tracks risks, and ensures stakeholder communication
- **Product Manager (PdM)**: Defines outcomes, prioritizes the backlog, and measures success metrics
- **Developers**: Implement features, collaborate on design, maintain quality standards, and identify technical risks
- **QA/Testing**: Validate quality, verify acceptance criteria, and conduct smoke tests before release

For detailed role descriptions, see [Roles and Personas](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md).

## Communication & Cadence

OctoAcme teams maintain consistent communication across multiple touchpoints:

- **Daily standups** (15 min) — Focus on progress, blockers, and dependencies
- **Weekly delivery sync** — Show progress, discuss flagged risks, and coordinate cross-team dependencies
- **Weekly PM + PdM alignment** — Discuss roadmap, metrics, and stakeholder updates
- **Sprint/milestone reviews** — Demo completed work and gather feedback
- **Monthly stakeholder updates** — Report progress against success metrics and key risks
- **Retrospectives** — Quarterly or milestone-based reflection on what went well and what to improve

## Key Artifacts & Templates

Throughout your projects, you'll use a variety of artifacts to communicate status, plan work, and track decisions:

- **Project Charter / One-pager** — Defines problem, goal, success metrics, stakeholders, and timeline
- **Roadmap and Release Plan** — High-level timeline for deliverables and milestones
- **Sprint/Iteration Backlog** — Prioritized work items with acceptance criteria
- **Risk Register** — Tracked risks with impact, likelihood, mitigation plans, and status
- **Definition of Done** — Shared understanding of what "complete" means for a feature
- **Release Notes** — Summary of changes, migration steps, and known issues
- **Retrospective Notes** — Action items and learnings captured after sprints or releases
